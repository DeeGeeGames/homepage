- `deegee-games.svg`: supplied DeeGee Games brand lockup, copied from Math Marsh's `src/assets/brand/deegee-games.svg`. Outlined Montserrat lettering; the supplied font notice is retained in `deegee-games-OFL.txt`.
- `../public/favicon.svg`: code-native extraction of the symbol from the supplied DeeGee Games SVG, on the site's charcoal background.
- `../public/social-card.png`: supplied DeeGee Games gradient artwork, copied from Math Marsh's `src/assets/brand/deegee-games-gradient.png`.
- `all-or-nothing.jpg`: published All or Nothing Steam header, downloaded on 2026-10-20 from https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/4537590/954227cc7543cfb6ceca561e3efd483fdd77d162/header.jpg?t=1792555268 . Store page: https://store.steampowered.com/app/4537590/ .
- `math-marsh.webp`: updated Math Marsh banner from https://github.com/DeeGeeGames/math-marsh/blob/master/src/assets/brand/math-marsh-banner.png , downloaded on 2026-10-23, resized to 1100 × 513 pixels and compressed to WebP for delivery; composition retained.
- `power-up.webp`: published Power Up gameplay thumbnail, copied unchanged on 2026-10-29 from https://experiments.deegeegames.com/images/power-up.webp .
- `steam.svg`: Steam icon from Simple Icons, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/steam.svg . CC0 notice retained in `simple-icons-LICENSE.md`. The original path is retained with the fill set to the button foreground color. Steam and its logo belong to Valve.
- Browser, source, and playground flask icons: small inline code-native SVG shapes in `index.html`.

- `reactionator.webp`: development screenshot captured from Reactionator for its homepage card.
